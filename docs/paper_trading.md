# ASTRA FUSION QUANT - Paper Trading Guide

## Why Paper Trade First?
The settings provided in this project (e.g., Score Floor = 75, Cooldown = 5) are **unvalidated hypotheses**. Before risking real capital, you must establish statistical significance through paper trading.

## How to Paper Trade on TradingView
1. Load `astra_strategy.pine` onto your TradingView chart.
2. In the bottom panel, click **Trading Panel**.
3. Select **Paper Trading by TradingView**.
4. Set your initial balance to match your intended real-world capital.
5. The strategy will automatically place paper trades according to the Astra signals.
6. Monitor the Strategy Tester tab for net profit, max drawdown, and win rate.
